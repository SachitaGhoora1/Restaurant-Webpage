Our Story

Nestled in the heart of Mauritius, Island Spice Heaven is a culinary sanctuary that brings together the vibrant flavors of the island. This charming restaurant celebrates the rich cultural heritage of Mauritian cuisine, blending Creole, Indian, Chinese, and French influences into every dish.

Menu

From the moment you step in, the menu features a delightful array of traditional dishes, including Rougaille saucisse (a flavorful tomato-based sausage stew), Octopus curry infused with coconut milk, and the beloved Dholl Puri, served with spicy chutneys. Seafood lovers can indulge in freshly grilled fish seasoned with local herbs, while vegetarians can savor comforting lentil-based dishes.
